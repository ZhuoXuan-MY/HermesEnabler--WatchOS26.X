## Impact on the Hermès Circulaire blocker

The original `NTKFaceBundleManager` normal lookup route calls `isRestrictedForDevice:`, and if restriction is reported, consults the newly identified **`_CLKIsFaceSnapshotService`** from ClockKit. The two lookup call sites were already verified independently at `0x544B0F9C` and `0x544B1174`.

**Strong, evidence-based interpretation:** This is a snapshot-rendering/service exception path, **not evidence of a user-accessible universal Hermès entitlement override**. Its exact process-detection condition is not yet decoded; do not assert the live watch return value from a static name alone.

Separately, `_NTKInternalBuild` explains the earlier V16 `NTKInternalBuild` link. V16's real watch result was false for the relevant internal/hardware-face flags. It is an independent predicate and must not be conflated with the snapshot-service exception.

The Poodle restriction from V25 remains `restricted=true` for measured Ultra 1 inputs, and `_NTKCanAddWatchFace` contains separate configuration, descriptor availability and face-class restriction checks. Neither the snapshot service nor the migration-specific lookup proves the ordinary Add-to-Library route works.
